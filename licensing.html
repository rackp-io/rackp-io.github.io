Licensing

The RACKP specification is openly published, and the reference implementations are source-available. All use under versions prior to v1.0.0 is free of charge, including commercial operation.

No license fee

  • All use under versions prior to v1.0.0.
  • Keeper (K) node operation and anchoring, at any version.
  • Deployments not accessible to parties outside the operator's own organization (on-premises, VPN, air-gapped), regardless of volume.

From v1.0.0

Referee (R) operators pay a license fee of USD 1.00 per billable artifact (CONTRIBUTION_RESULT or POH_CERTIFICATE). The first 100 artifacts per operator are free. No application or approval is required to operate a Referee commercially.

Operators who wish to continue beyond 100 artifacts without a fee (for example, academic research or registered non-profit use) can submit an exemption request before exceeding the threshold. The LICENSE is the authoritative text.

Supporting RACKP

Organizations that would like to support protocol development, or that need a formal licensing certification for enterprise or procurement purposes, are welcome to get in touch. Sponsorship terms are published on the Sponsor Terms page.
